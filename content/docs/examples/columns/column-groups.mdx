Three groups sit above the columns: Contact, Work, and Standing. Work holds a nested History
group, so the header has three rows. Name is pinned left, so the Contact header splits at the pin
edge. Click a group header to select its columns. Drag a group header to move the block, or drag a
column inside its group.

## What's in the demo

- `columnGroups`: a list of `{ id, header, children }` groups. `children` holds column ids and nested groups
- A group header press selects the group's columns
- A group header drag moves the block inside its parent group
- The columns menu lists the members of each group under the group label
