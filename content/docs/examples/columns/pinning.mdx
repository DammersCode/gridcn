The column surface in one grid: Name is pinned left and Score pinned right by default, Email
opts out of resizing, and the long Bio column is a `flex: 1` autosize target. Pinning, resize,
reorder, and visibility are all on by default and adjustable per column (`pinnable`,
`resizable`, `reorderable`) or grid-wide (`enableColumnPinning`, etc.).

## What's in the demo

- `pin: "left"` / `pin: "right"` — pinned bands with the frozen-edge shadow
- `resizable: false` on Email; drag another header's edge to resize
- `flex: 1` on Bio — double-click a header edge to autosize to its content
- Header dropdown (hover a header for the ghost chevron, or right-click) — pin/unpin, autosize
- Toolbar columns menu — hide a column, then restore it the same way
