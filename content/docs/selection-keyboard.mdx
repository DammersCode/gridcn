## Selection model

A selection has an **anchor cell** (the active cell, Excel's white cell) and a rectangular
**range** around it. With multi-range enabled, it also has a **stack** of additional
ctrl-clicked ranges. Rows and columns also have their own whole-row and whole-column selection
channels, through marker-column checkbox clicks and header clicks, independent of the range.

Checked rows follow their records when a sort, filter, or new `data` array rebuilds the view.
When a row leaves the view, the grid unchecks it. Row ids must be unique: when two rows share an
id, the check follows the first row with that id. The active cell follows its record and its column
when the view rebuilds and when columns move, hide, or pin. When its row or its column leaves the
view, the active cell keeps its view position, clamped into the view. When a rebuild puts other
records or other columns under the range, the grid collapses the range onto the active cell. A
clamp that moves the active cell also collapses the selection onto it. A clamp that keeps the
active cell in place drops the range. A row operation never reaches a record that you did not select.
Without checked rows or a range, a row operation acts on the active cell's row. Selected columns
follow their column when columns move, hide, or pin, and a column that leaves the view leaves the
selection.

Selection overlays are placed on grid lines and render with zero cell re-renders. Dragging a
selection never re-renders a single cell.

## Mouse gestures

- **Plain left-button press and drag** from any cell, active or not, live-paints a rectangular
  range from the press origin to the hovered cell, in any direction.
- **Shift+click** extends the range from the current anchor to the clicked cell.
- **Header press and drag** selects a contiguous multi-column range. A plain header click
  selects the whole column, or sorts it if `headerClickBehavior="sort"`.
- **Marker press and drag**, with `rowMarkers` enabled, selects multiple rows. The range follows
  the pointer from the press row, so dragging back over selected rows de-selects them again. In
  the `reorder`-family marker modes only a drag that starts on the drag handle zone reorders the row, and
  it leaves the selection as it was. Drags from the number or checkbox glyph still select.
- **Ctrl/Cmd-click** adds a new range to the stack (`enableMultiRange`, on by default).

The grid body suppresses native browser text selection during these gestures. Editors remain
normally text-selectable.

## Touch gestures

<NewBadge />

A touch screen scrolls the grid natively. The grid reads the input type of each press, so a laptop
with a touch screen and a trackpad gets touch gestures from the screen and mouse gestures from the
trackpad.

| Gesture | Result |
|---|---|
| Tap a cell | Selects the cell. A scroll or a pan selects nothing. |
| Double-tap a cell | Starts editing, or toggles a checkbox cell. A read-only grid does not edit. |
| Tap a header | Selects the column, or sorts it with `headerClickBehavior="sort"`. |
| Tap a row marker | Selects the row. |
| Long-press (hold for 500 ms) | Opens the context menu on the cell or the header. |
| Drag a selection handle | Extends the range from the opposite corner. The grid scrolls at the edges. |

After a touch selection, two selection handles appear at the top-start and bottom-end corners of
the range. Any key press hides them. A press must stay within 10 px to count as a tap or a
long-press.

The grid sets `touch-action: manipulation`. A double-tap does not zoom the page, and pinch zoom
stays available. Use `density="comfortable"` for rows that are easier to hit with a finger.

## Configurability

You can disable each selection channel independently through `DataGridProvider` props. All
default to `true`:

```tsx
<DataGridProvider
  enableRowSelection={true}
  enableColumnSelection={true}
  enableRangeSelection={true}
  enableMultiRange={true}
  {...grid}
  columns={columns}
>
```

<Callout title="Disabling a channel removes its state, not just its gestures">
  `enableColumnSelection={false}` makes header clicks do nothing, rather than only hiding a
  highlight. The column selection channel itself is gone.
</Callout>

## Checked rows

<NewBadge />

A checked row is a row whose marker checkbox is on. A click outside the grid, or in the empty space of the grid, clears the cell range and the column selection. It never clears checked rows. A toolbar button outside the grid reads the checked rows after it is clicked.

Selecting a cell clears the checked rows. Set `keepCheckedRows` to keep them:

```tsx
<DataGridProvider keepCheckedRows {...grid} columns={columns}>
```

With `keepCheckedRows`, a cell click, an arrow key, and a Shift or Ctrl click leave the checked rows as they are. Escape, the marker checkboxes, the header checkbox, `clearSelection()`, and deleting a row change them.

While at least one row is checked, row operations act only on the checked rows, never on the range. This covers Delete rows, Duplicate row, Move rows, and the row items of the cell context menu. Cell operations such as copy, cut, fill, and Delete stay on the cell range. With no checked row, row operations use the range.

| Prop | Type | Default |
|---|---|---|
| `keepCheckedRows` <NewBadge /> | `boolean` | `false` |

## Header click behavior

`headerClickBehavior` on `DataGridProvider` picks what a **plain** header click does. Header
press and drag always multi-selects columns, regardless of this setting.

| Value | A plain header click... |
|---|---|
| `"select"` (default) | Selects the whole column, through the column selection channel. |
| `"sort"` | Cycles that column's sort between ascending, descending, and clear, instead of selecting. See [Sorting, filtering & search](/docs/sorting-filtering-search#sorting). |
| `"none"` | Does nothing. Use this when you drive sort exclusively through [`data-grid-sort-list`](/docs/sorting-filtering-search#sort-list-toolbar-add-on) or your own UI, and you do not want header clicks to also select or sort. |

```tsx
<DataGridProvider headerClickBehavior="none" {...grid} columns={columns}>
```

Try all three values live in the [playground](/docs/playground).

Per-column `sortable: false` still removes that one column from the click-to-sort gesture, even
under `headerClickBehavior="sort"`. You can still sort it programmatically through
`useDataGridActions().setSorts()`.

## Keyboard map

The keymap is a plain data table, the `keymap` prop, merged over `DEFAULT_KEYMAP`. You can remap
or extend any binding without touching the engine. The remapping surface and the in-app
shortcuts dialog are on the [Keybindings](/docs/addons/keybindings) page. The table below is generated
from the shipped defaults.

`mod` resolves to <kbd>Cmd</kbd> on macOS and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> elsewhere. Copy, Cut, and Paste
(<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>Cmd</kbd>+C/X/V) are native browser clipboard events, not part of the
keymap, but the list above includes them because the shortcuts dialog shows them together.

<Callout type="warn" title="Escape outside edit mode clears the whole selection">
  The <kbd>Esc</kbd> row is labeled "Cancel edit", but with no editor open the `cancel` action
  clears the entire selection: the cell range and its stack, plus any whole-row and whole-column
  selection. To keep a selection across an Escape, remap or drop the `cancel` binding through the
  `keymap` prop.
</Callout>

## Undo and redo outside the grid

The in-grid undo and redo bindings above work only while focus is inside the grid.
[Global shortcuts](/docs/global-shortcuts) extends them to a custom toolbar or
anywhere else on the page: mount `DataGridGlobalShortcuts` inside `DataGridRoot` and the same
bindings reach the grid while focus is outside.

## Checking rows by keyboard

The marker checkboxes are not tab stops, so the keyboard has its own actions:

- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Space</kbd> (`toggleRow`) checks or unchecks the active
  row. The other checked rows stay checked.
- <kbd>Mod</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>E</kbd> (`toggleAllRows`) checks every row, or unchecks
  them when every row is checked. It does what the header checkbox does.
- <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Space</kbd> (`selectRow`) replaces the selection with the active row.

To check rows that are not next to each other, move with <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Arrow</kbd>, which
keeps the selection, and press `toggleRow` on each row. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>Cmd</kbd>+A
(`selectAll`) selects cells, not checked rows.

## Moving rows

<NewBadge />

<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>PageUp</kbd> (`moveRowsUp`) and
<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>PageDown</kbd> (`moveRowsDown`) move the checked rows one place.
When no row is checked, they move the rows of the selected range, or the active row. They need a
`reorder`-family `rowMarkers` mode. See [Row operations](/docs/row-operations#reorder).

## Opening the column filter

<NewBadge />

<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>ArrowUp</kbd> (`openColumnFilter`) opens the header filter of the
active cell's column. It does nothing unless the root has a `renderHeaderFilter` slot and the column offers a set filter.
The <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>ArrowDown</kbd> chord opens the column menu.

## Two-stage Ctrl+A

<Callout title="Two-stage select-all">
  The first <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>Cmd</kbd>+A selects the current data region. Press it again to
  select the whole grid.
</Callout>
