gridcn is **source-available**, not open source (OSI). The full source is public and free to
use, but the license restricts commercial redistribution of gridcn itself. The legal text is
[`LICENSE`](https://github.com/DammersCode/gridcn/blob/main/LICENSE) (GridCN Source Available
License 1.0).

## What you may do

- Use gridcn in personal, internal, and **commercial** products — websites, apps, SaaS.
- Modify the code and keep your changes private. Your product does not have to be open source.
- Sell or monetize your own products that use gridcn.
- Fork the repository for personal, educational, or internal use.
- Contribute to the official repository through pull requests.

## What needs written permission

- Selling or licensing gridcn (or a substantially similar derivative) as a standalone
  component library, design system, or registry.
- Running a monetized registry, marketplace, or hosted distribution service whose primary
  purpose is to provide gridcn to third parties.
- Rebranding gridcn and offering the same collection of components as a competing library.
- Selling access to gridcn itself, where gridcn is the primary value of the offering.

Incorporating gridcn into your own product, including a commercial SaaS, is never restricted.
If gridcn is only a small part of an offering that is primarily your own original work, the
redistribution restrictions do not apply.

## Attribution

If you use gridcn in a commercial product, include a reasonable credit — for example in an
"About", "Credits", or "Acknowledgements" section — that names gridcn as the source of the
components and links to the official repository where technically possible. No prominent
branding is required, and attribution does not imply endorsement.

When you distribute any part of the code, you must include a copy of the license and the
copyright notice.

## Commercial licensing

Uses that are restricted above can be licensed. Contact the maintainers through the
[official repository](https://github.com/DammersCode/gridcn).
