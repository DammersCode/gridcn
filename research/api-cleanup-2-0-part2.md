# API cleanup for 2.0, part 2

Second scan of the public API of `registry/default/blocks/**` and `content/docs`, split into five areas: core props, core interaction, IO and clipboard, data sources, UI add-ons. It follows `api-cleanup-2-0.md` (round 1) and the docs-session items already accepted (4 controlled-data dev warning, 5 `text-end` span inheritance, 6 `history.record` returns `false` on `readOnly`, 7 a plugin keymap binds the plugin's own commands, 8 `updateCells({ flash })`, 10 `onSave` on the provider only). Items 1, 2, 3, 9, 11 and 12 of that docs-session list are rejected and not re-opened, as are round 1 items 1-12, D1-D8, F1-F22 and the skip list (`keepCheckedRows`, `keepHiddenCheckedRows`, `checkedRowsLifetime`).

Every kept item verifies in code, gives a real gain in clarity, cleanliness or prop count, and loses nothing. Each keeps a default, an override, a manual equivalent and, where policies differ, a policy hook. File:line evidence, refutation and blast radius per item live in the five reviewer files; this document is the merged decision list.

Counts: 65 proposals across the five reviewers. 59 kept after dedupe (6 merged away). About 104 rejected. About 20 kept items need an owner decision (section 5). Kept per area (a merged row counts under its first area):
